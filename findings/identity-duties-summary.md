# When does Utah law require identifying a person? — summary

**Verdict on the proposition: substantially accurate, with two refinements.**

## How this was derived

Two corpora were retrieved in full on 2026-07-28: all **96 titles of the Utah Code** (statute, from
le.utah.gov, as version-stamped XML) and all **2,294 current rules of the Utah Administrative Code**
(adminrules.utah.gov).

**Rules** are administrative rules — binding law made by agencies under authority the legislature
delegates in statute, adopted through the Utah Administrative Rulemaking Act (Title 63G, Ch. 3) and
published by the Office of Administrative Rules. They carry legal force and hold operative detail the
statute omits, which is why statute alone is insufficient: the fishing-licence identity requirements
exist only at the rule layer.

This was **not** an exhaustive reading of ~2,400 instruments. Conclusions rest on full-text search of
both corpora for identity-verification phrase families, plus close reading of the rules for wildlife
licensing (R657), Medicaid (R414) and public assistance (R986). A requirement hiding in an unexamined
family cannot be excluded.

## Where no identification is required

Fishing licence — **§23A-4-601(2)**: "*Upon paying the fee prescribed by the Wildlife Board, a person
may obtain a license to fish.*" No identity element. Misrepresentation is deterred afterward by
**§23A-4-1101** (class B misdemeanour), not prevented by a gate.

**Refinement 1 — attribute collection *is* mandatory.** **R657-45-2(2)**: forms "*shall include the
licensee's customer identification number, name, date of birth, address, height, weight, eye color,
hair color, gender.*" An agency failing to gather these would be out of compliance. Absent is any
duty to *validate* them. Precisely: **identity assertion** is required; **identity proofing** —
correlating the person to an authoritative identity record — is not.

The general benefits provision shows the same design. **§63G-12-402** verifies *lawful presence*, a
status, not identity; applies only to applicants 18 or older; and prescribes its method at **(4)(a)**
— applicants "*certify under penalty of perjury.*" The SAVE check at **(5)** is triggered only by
(4)(a)(ii), the non-citizen branch, so for a citizen signing the form is the whole procedure. **(3)**
exempts emergency care, immunisations, shelter and retirement benefits.

## Where stronger identification is required

**Refinement 2 — these exist, and dereliction there genuinely is non-compliance.**

- **Medicaid — R414-308-4(4)**: where citizenship and identity "*do not match through the Social
  Security electronic match process,*" the applicant must "*provide verification of his citizenship
  and identity*" or "*original documentation*" within 90 days (implementing 42 U.S.C. §1396a(ee)).
- **Child care assistance — R986-700-702(3)(a)**: "*A client must verify identity.*"
- **Lost lifetime hunting/fishing licence — R657-17-8(1)(a)**: duplicate issued on "*providing
  verification of identity.*"

## The dividing line

Not compliance, but programme type. These duties arrive as **federal conditions on federally funded,
means-tested benefits** (42 C.F.R. §435.406/407), not as a Utah-wide rule, and run
electronic-match-first with documentary proof only on mismatch. Assurance is set programme by
programme — nothing, attestation, or match-with-fallback. **Utah has no general identity-assurance
baseline.**
